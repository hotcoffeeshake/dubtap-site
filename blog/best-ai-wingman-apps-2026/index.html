2. Smoothspeak: best if you like a keyboard

Smoothspeak's App Store listing describes an AI keyboard that suggests replies inside dating apps and messaging apps, and can also work from screenshots. It says it remembers past conversations with each person.

Trade-offs: you switch to its keyboard to use it, which means granting a third-party keyboard access to what you type.

3. WingAI: best for dating-app openers

WingAI's listing positions it for Tinder, Hinge and Bumble: upload a screenshot of a profile or a chat and get suggested lines.

Trade-offs: it's an upload flow, so you leave the chat each time.

4. Rizz: best for pickup-line ideas

One of the best-known names in the category, built around generating lines from a screenshot.

Trade-offs: when we checked in November 2026, its App Store listing had not been updated since January 2026. Line-focused output can read like pickup lines rather than conversation.

5. Gleam: best for general social-skills lessons

Gleam is a lessons app ("Social Intelligence") covering conversation, confidence and work situations, in a short-lesson format.

Trade-offs: it teaches; it doesn't help with the message in front of you right now.

6. ChatGPT: best for long, unusual situations

A general chatbot can talk through a complicated situation in depth, and you may already have it.

Trade-offs: you copy the chat, paste it, explain the backstory every time, and the answers tend to come out long and formal. It doesn't remember each person unless you set that up.

How to choose

  • Will you use it in the moment? The best app is the one you can open in 2 seconds while the chat is on screen.
  • Do you want to keep your keyboard? If not installing a third-party keyboard matters to you, pick an app that doesn't need one.
  • Does it explain itself? Replies with a reason teach you something; replies without one keep you dependent.
  • Is the trial honest? Check the price, the trial length and whether you get a reminder before you're charged.
  • What happens to your screenshots? Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

What is the best AI wingman app in 2026?

It depends on how you want to use it. If you want help without leaving the chat or installing a keyboard, Dubtap (iPhone, coming soon) is built for that. If you prefer a keyboard, Smoothspeak; for dating-app openers from a screenshot, WingAI; for structured lessons, Gleam.

Do AI wingman apps need a keyboard?

Many do: they work by replacing your keyboard and asking for "Full Access". Others ask you to upload screenshots. Dubtap uses iPhone Back Tap instead, so no keyboard is needed.

Is it safe to upload chat screenshots to an AI app?

Read the privacy policy first. Check whether screenshots are stored, which AI provider processes them, and whether you can delete your data. Remember the other person's messages are in there too.
