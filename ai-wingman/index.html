FAQ

What is an AI wingman?

An app that reads your conversation and helps you with the next message: what to say, what her message means, and when to suggest meeting up. Dubtap is an AI wingman for iPhone that you call up by double-tapping the back of your phone.

Is using an AI wingman weird?

It's like asking a friend who's good at texting, just faster. You still choose and send every message, and you still show up for the date. Dubtap explains each reply, so over time you need it less.

Does it work on Hinge, Tinder and Bumble?

Yes. It works on top of any chat screen, including dating apps, iMessage, Instagram DMs and WhatsApp.

Do I have to install a keyboard?

No. Most AI texting apps ask you to switch to their keyboard and grant it "Full Access". Dubtap uses Apple's Back Tap and a Shortcut instead, so you keep your normal keyboard, autocorrect and emoji.

What happens to my chats?

Dubtap asks your permission before the first reply. The screenshot is processed to read the text and is not stored on our servers; the text is kept in your history so replies fit the conversation, and you can delete a person or your whole account anytime. No ad or tracking SDKs. Details in our Privacy Policy.

Is there an Android version?

Dubtap launches on iPhone first. Back Tap requires iPhone 8 or newer with iOS 14 or later.
