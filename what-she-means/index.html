What "haha" means

Usually: she read it and liked it, but didn't give you anything to work with.

How to tell: If it's "haha" plus something else (a question, an emoji, a comeback), she's engaged. If "haha" is the whole reply to your question, she has nothing to add, and asking another question will feel like an interview.

Her
haha
What to text back
Okay, new topic: best thing you ate this week?
I'll take that as you agreeing with me
Alright, I owe you a better joke in person. Coffee Thursday?

Don't: send "haha" back, or apologize for the joke.

What "maybe another time" means

Usually: either she's genuinely busy, or it's a polite no.

How to tell: A busy person suggests the other time ("maybe next week?"). A polite no doesn't. Neither one is a reason to push.

Her
maybe another time
What to text back
No worries. What does your next week look like?
Sounds good, you pick the day next time

Don't: ask "why?", guilt-trip, or keep inviting. If it's "maybe" twice with no other time offered, let it go.

What "still figuring it out" means

Usually: her plans are open, and she may be leaving space for you to suggest something.

How to tell: If she said it after you asked "what are you up to this weekend", it's an opening. If she adds details ("might see friends"), she's telling you she's booked.

Her
still figuring it out
What to text back
Then I'll plan it. Saturday afternoon, you just show up.
Figuring it out is the fun part. Want a hint?
Let me guess: brunch, then pretending you'll be productive

Don't: ask "what do you want to do?". She just told you she doesn't know. Lead.

What "lol" means

Usually: polite acknowledgement. Lowercase "lol" alone is the text version of a small smile.

How to tell: Same rule as "haha": look at what comes with it. "LOL" in capitals or "lmaooo" is a real laugh.

Her
lol
What to text back
Glad I could get a polite lol out of you. Next goal: a real one
Anyway, how did the thing you mentioned go?

Don't: explain the joke.

What "I'm so busy this week" means

Usually: she's busy, and it says nothing either way about interest until you see what comes next.

How to tell: Interested and busy sounds like "this week is crazy but next week works". Not interested sounds like the busy message, every week.

Her
I'm so busy this week
What to text back
Sounds intense. Good luck with it. Let's do something next week when it calms down?
Go crush it. I'll check back Sunday

Don't: text every day "just checking in". Give her the week.

FAQ

What does it mean when she replies "haha"?

It depends on what came before. After a joke, it's usually genuine. As the whole reply to a question, it often means she has nothing to add. Change direction or suggest a plan instead of asking another question.

What does "maybe another time" mean?

Either she's busy or she's politely saying no. If she offers another time, she's busy. If she doesn't, ask once more lightly; if it's "maybe" again, let it go.

What does "still figuring it out" mean when you ask about her plans?

Her plans are open, and she may be giving you room to suggest something. Make a specific, easy plan.

How do I know if she's interested over text?

Look at effort, not words: does she ask questions back, add new topics, and agree to specific plans? One dry message means little; a pattern over several days means a lot.
