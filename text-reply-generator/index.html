FAQ

What should I text when she just says "haha"?

Add something new instead of matching her energy. "haha" after a joke is a green light to keep going; "haha" as a full reply to a question usually means she has nothing to add, so change the topic or suggest a plan. Dubtap reads the messages before it and tells you which one it is. More examples in What "haha" means.

What do I reply when she says "maybe another time"?

Make the next move easy and low-pressure: "No worries. What does your week look like?" If she says it twice without offering another time, take the hint and let it go. See What "maybe another time" means.

How do I reply to "still figuring it out"?

She is handing you the plan. Don't ask what she wants to do; suggest something specific: "Then I'll plan it. Saturday afternoon, you just show up."

Is there an AI that replies to texts for you?

Dubtap suggests replies; it never sends anything for you. You double-tap the back of your iPhone, it reads the chat on screen, and 3 replies appear at the top with the first one already copied. You pick, edit if you want, and send it yourself.

Does it work with iMessage, Instagram, Hinge and WhatsApp?

Yes. Because you trigger it from the back of your phone, it works on top of any chat app: iMessage, Instagram, Hinge, Tinder, Bumble, WhatsApp, Snapchat and more.

Can it read a screenshot?

You don't need to take one. When you double-tap, the Shortcut captures the screen for you. You can also import older chat screenshots into a person's profile so Dubtap knows your history with her.

Does it learn how I text?

Tell Dubtap a bit about yourself and the replies sound more like you. It also keeps one profile per person, so replies fit her and where the two of you are.
