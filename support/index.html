Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Contact us

Email support@dubtap.app. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Set up the double-tap

  1. Open Dubtap and follow the one-time setup to add the shortcut.
  2. On your iPhone, go to Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap, and choose the Dubtap shortcut.
  3. Open any chat and double-tap the back of your iPhone. Replies appear at the top of the screen in about 20–40 seconds, with the first one already copied.

Cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Dubtap → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Request a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose Dubtap.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, people profiles, and saved chats. You can also email support@dubtap.app from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Report a reply

Every reply has a "Report" link. Use it if a suggestion is inappropriate, and we will review it.
