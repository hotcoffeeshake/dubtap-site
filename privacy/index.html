Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what Dubtap collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Contact: support@dubtap.app.

What we collect

  • Account: a guest ID tied to your device, or your email address if you sign in with email. Your plan status and usage counts.
  • Content you choose to share: chat screenshots and messages you send to Dubtap for analysis. These may include messages written by the other person in the chat. Text read from a screenshot is saved to your history so replies can fit the conversation; the screenshot image itself is processed and not stored on our servers.
  • People profiles: details you add about someone you're texting (nickname, stage, personality, likes) and AI-written summaries of your chats with them.
  • Waitlist: if you join the waitlist on our website, your email address, the page you signed up on, the referring site and your browser language. We use it only to email you about Dubtap's launch and your offer code, and we don't share it with anyone except Supabase, which stores it. Email support@dubtap.app to be removed.
  • Feedback: replies you use or report, and feedback you send us.
  • Usage events: basic in-app events (for example, which screens you open) sent to our own servers to improve the app. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Website analytics: our website (not the app) uses Google Analytics to count visits, see which pages people read and which sites sent them. It sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device details; we don't use it for advertising and we don't send it your name or email. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies.
  • Purchases: subscription status from Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment card.

How we use it

To generate replies and explanations, keep your people profiles, run your subscription, keep the service safe (including reviewing reported replies), and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information.

AI processing

Before your first reply, the app asks for your permission to send your content for AI processing. When you request a reply, the screenshot or message is sent from our servers to an AI model provider to generate the result:

  • Anthropic (Claude models), accessed through a third-party API gateway service.

We send only what is needed to generate your reply. Contact us for details about these providers and how long they keep content.

Who we share data with

  • Amazon Web Services (United States): hosts our servers.
  • Supabase (United States, on AWS): sign-in, our database and the website waitlist.
  • Google (Google Analytics): website visit statistics.
  • AI providers listed above: only the content needed to generate your reply.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscriptions and purchases.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, history and people profiles until you delete them. Deleting a person removes their profile and saved chats. Deleting your account (in the app: Me → Delete account) permanently deletes your account and all related data from our database.

Your choices and rights

You can view and delete people profiles and your account in the app at any time. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data; we respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have additional rights; contact us to exercise them.

Other people's messages

Screenshots can contain messages from people who don't use Dubtap. Only share chats you're comfortable sharing, and avoid sharing sensitive information such as addresses, financial details or health information.

Children

Dubtap is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using Dubtap, contact us and we will delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our systems is limited to people who need it to run the service.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
